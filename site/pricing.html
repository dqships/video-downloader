What's included

Every format
MP4, WebM and MOV files, HLS and DASH streams, audio-only saves as M4A or MP3, and subtitles.
Every quality
Pick any quality the site offers, up to the highest it sends. Nothing is re-encoded.
Live streams and player recording
Record a live stream until you stop it, or record a player whose stream can't be fetched as a file.
No limits
No cap on how many videos you save, how long they are, or how big the files get. No watermark.
No account, no tracking
Vidzilla has no sign-in and contains no analytics code. Its ads are for our own apps and report nothing back.

Why it's free

Vidzilla runs entirely in your browser. Files come straight from the site that serves them to your computer, so there's no download server to pay for and no reason to charge you. The occasional ad for our other apps keeps it that way, and the ad-free upgrade is there if you'd rather not see them.

Get it

Add Vidzilla from the Chrome Web Store. It works in Chrome 116 and later on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS.
