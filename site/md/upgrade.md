## How paying works

Checkout is on Stripe, which takes your card and email and sends the receipt. We never see your card. Vidzilla's copy has a random ID that isn't linked to you; the purchase is tagged with it, and the extension asks our server once a day whether that ID has paid. The server only asks Stripe and answers yes or no. It keeps no records of its own.
