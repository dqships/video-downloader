# Vidzilla is free

$0 · No account · No limits

There is one version of Vidzilla and it costs nothing. You get every feature the moment you add it to Chrome, with nothing to sign up for. Every few downloads it shows a short ad for another app by the same maker.

| Plan | Price | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vidzilla | $0, forever | Every feature below, with a short ad every few downloads |
| Without ads | $0.99 a year, or $9 once | The same, with the ads turned off. Buy it from Vidzilla's Settings, under Ads ([how it works]({{origin}}/upgrade)) |

## What's included

- **Every format**: MP4, WebM and MOV files, HLS and DASH streams, audio-only saves as M4A or MP3, and subtitles.
- **Every quality**: pick any quality the site offers, up to the highest it sends. Nothing is re-encoded.
- **Live streams and player recording**: record a live stream until you stop it, or record a player whose stream can't be fetched as a file.
- **No limits**: no cap on how many videos you save, how long they are, or how big the files get. No watermark.
- **No account, no tracking**: Vidzilla has no sign-in and contains no analytics code. Its ads are for our own apps and report nothing back.

## Why it's free

Vidzilla runs entirely in your browser. Files come straight from the site that serves them to your computer, so there's no download server to pay for and no reason to charge you. The occasional ad for our other apps keeps it that way, and the ad-free upgrade is there if you'd rather not see them.

## Get it

Add Vidzilla from the [Chrome Web Store]({{storeUrl}}). It works in Chrome 116 and later on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS.
